# Color Palette — Complete Specifications

## PRIMARY COLORS

### Crimson (Primary Identity)
**Hex**: `#DC143C`  
**RGB**: 220, 20, 60  
**CMYK**: 0, 91, 73, 14  
**Usage**: 40-50% of any design  

**Use for**:
- All CTAs and primary buttons
- Logo (primary version)
- Headlines in transformation content
- Your signature and photo borders
- Power quotes
- Metrics and result numbers
- Interactive hover states

---

### Deep Teal (Secondary Structure)
**Hex**: `#0D7377`  
**RGB**: 13, 115, 119  
**CMYK**: 89, 3, 0, 53  
**Usage**: 20-25% of any design  

**Use for**:
- Standard text links (non-CTA)
- Navigation elements
- Process indicators
- Method/system content
- Secondary buttons

---

### Prussian Blue (Support/Depth)
**Hex**: `#14425A`  
**RGB**: 20, 66, 90  
**CMYK**: 78, 27, 0, 65  
**Usage**: 10-15% of any design  

**Use for**:
- Section dividers
- Footer elements
- Deep backgrounds
- Subtle accents

---

# Typography Specifications

## Font Families

### Primary (UI & Headings)
**Font**: Inter or Source Sans 3  
**Weights**: 400 (Regular), 700 (Bold), 800 (ExtraBold)  
**Free alternative**: Inter (Google Fonts)

### Secondary (Editorial/Long-form)
**Font**: Newsreader, EB Garamond, or Literata  
**Weights**: 400 (Regular), 700 (Bold)  
**Free alternative**: EB Garamond (Google Fonts)

---

## Typography Scale

| Element | Size | Weight | Color | Line Height |
|---------|------|--------|-------|-------------|
| H1 | 44-52px | 800 | Crimson or Ink | 1.2 |
| H2 | 32-36px | 700 | Crimson or Ink | 1.3 |
| H3 | 22-26px | 700 | Ink | 1.4 |
| Body | 17-19px | 400 | Ink | 1.6 |
| Small/Caption | 14-15px | 400 | Slate | 1.5 |

**Letter-spacing**:
- Headlines: 0 to +2%
- Body: 0%
- UI Labels: +2-3%
- Small Caps: +10%

# Logo Specifications

## Primary Logo (Full Crimson)
**Color**: Entire logo in Crimson `#DC143C`  
**Minimum size**: 16px (digital), 24mm (print)  
**Clearspace**: 1.5× seed diameter around mark  
**Use**: Primary version for maximum impact

## Elemental Logo (Multi-color)
**Outer ring**: Deep Teal `#0D7377`  
**River flow**: Prussian Blue `#14425A`  
**Seed (center)**: Crimson `#DC143C`  
**Use**: When multi-color context needed

## Reversed Logo (Light on Dark)
**Color**: White `#FFFFFF`  
**Background**: Crimson `#DC143C` or dark backgrounds  
**Use**: Dark mode, Crimson backgrounds, photos

## Monochrome Logo (Minimal)
**Color**: Ink `#0E1B2A`  
**Use**: Very small sizes (below 16px), single-color contexts

# Spacing & Layout

## Grid System

**Base unit**: 8px  
**Scale**: 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96, 128px  

**Use**:
- All margins, padding, gaps use multiples of 8
- Maintains visual rhythm
- Creates professional consistency

---

## Common Spacing

| Element | Spacing |
|---------|---------|
| Between paragraphs | 24px |
| Between sections | 64px or 96px |
| Card padding | 24-32px |
| Button padding | 18px × 36px |
| Line height (body) | 1.6 |
| Line height (headings) | 1.2-1.4 |

---

## Border Radius

**Sharp/Modern**: 6px  
**Soft cards**: 12-16px  
**Brutalist/Power**: 0px (sharp corners)  

**Use**:
- Buttons: 6px or 0px
- Cards: 12-16px
- Images: 0px (keep sharp)

# Accessibility Standards

## Contrast Ratios (WCAG AAA)

**Passing combinations**:
- White on Crimson `#DC143C` → 5.3:1 (AA, large text AAA)
- Ink `#0E1B2A` on Ivory `#FFFFF0` → 15.1:1 (AAA)
- Ink `#0E1B2A` on Bone `#E7D9C4` → 11.8:1 (AAA)
- White on Deep Teal `#0D7377` → 7.4:1 (AAA)

**Use with caution**:
- Crimson `#DC143C` text on light backgrounds → 4.8:1 (AA, use 18px+ or bold)

**Never use**:
- Crimson text on Deep Teal (fails contrast)
- Deep Teal text on Crimson (fails contrast)
- Slate `#7B8BA5` on Crimson (fails contrast)

---

## Focus States

**Focus ring**: 3px solid Crimson `#DC143C`  
**Offset**: 2px white space between element and ring  
**Use**: All interactive elements (buttons, links, form fields)

# Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Don't**:
- Use more than 3 colors in a single design
- Place Crimson text on Deep Teal or Prussian Blue
- Use pastel or muted versions of colors
- Add heavy shadows or gradients
- Use Crimson for body text (only headlines, CTAs, accents)
- Forget whitespace (let Crimson breathe)
- Use script or decorative fonts

**Do**:
- Lead with Crimson (40-50% of design)
- Maintain generous whitespace
- Use high contrast for readability
- Keep layouts clean and structured
- Make CTAs obviously Crimson
- Test on multiple devices
- Follow the 8px spacing grid
