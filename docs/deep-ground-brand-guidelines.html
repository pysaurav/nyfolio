brand guidelines · v1.0 · august 2026

Deep ground, amplified.

The complete identity system: one dark ground, one loud voice, three disciplined details. Every rule in this document is demonstrated by the document itself.

01 — foundation

The idea

Quiet surface, voltage underneath.

This identity belongs to a strategist: someone whose work is choosing, cutting, and committing — and whose brand should feel the same way. The system is built on a deliberate tension. The ground is calm, warm, and crafted. The voice is scarlet and unapologetic. The discipline holding the two together is what makes it read senior rather than loud.

The archetype is the trusted strategist, not the loud visionary. This brand wins in the room and in writing — one-to-one persuasion and long-form depth — so every asset optimizes for weight per word, not volume.

Five principles

The ground stays calm

Bone and ink carry 80% of every surface. Warmth and depth come first; accents arrive later and leave early.

Scarlet is budgeted

One highlighted word, one button, or one hard claim per surface. Scarcity is what keeps it powerful.

Type is the imagery

No stock photography. Oversized serif, color blocks, and charts do the visual work.

One broken rule per layout

Every composition may break exactly one convention — an overflowing word, an inverted highlight. One, never three.

Depth over frequency

One heavyweight essay beats thirty posts. The content cadence is part of the brand.

Everything is consistent

The same five colors, four type voices, and one monogram, everywhere, without exception. Recognition compounds.

04 — typography

Four voices

Every piece of text belongs to exactly one voice. All faces are free on Google Fonts, so the system runs anywhere at no cost.

Machine — Fraunces 600

Headlines and claims. 72px hero, 48px section, 28px heading. Line-height 1.1, slightly tight tracking. The serif is huge or absent — mid-sized bold headlines are banned.

Human — Fraunces italic 400

One aside per page. The warm counterweight — a personal note, a wry admission, a dedication.

Working — Space Grotesk 400 / 500

Body text and interface. 17px, line-height 1.65, maximum 68 characters per line. 500 for emphasis and buttons — never bolder.

Data — JetBrains Mono 400

Dates, labels, numbers, metadata, code. 12–13px. Sentence case always — never uppercase, never letter-spaced into a whisper.

06 — graphic elements

The signatures

The three-band mark

Ink, scarlet, gold — stacked in the 5:2:1 ratio. Used as the LinkedIn banner base, document divider, and closing element. It is the palette compressed into a flag.

The highlight

Strategy is subtraction.

One word per headline. On dark surfaces, invert: bone block, scarlet word.

Charts

Teal carries the story, scarlet marks the failure or turning point, gold annotates. Mono labels, hairline gridlines, ink background. Charts are hero imagery — designed, not defaulted.

07 — imagery and icons

Almost none, on purpose

No stock photography, ever. Type, color blocks, the monogram, and charts carry all visual weight. Where a photograph is unavoidable — press, speaker pages — it receives a duotone treatment in ink and bone, and the monogram never sits on top of it.

Icons are thin outlines only: 1.5px stroke, square caps, drawn on a 24px grid, colored in the current text color. Never filled, never multicolor, never decorative.

09 — voice and content

Say less, mean more

The visual boldness must be matched in writing or the system collapses. The register is declarative, precise, and unafraid of a hard opinion — with unusually rigorous work underneath. The gap between the loud shell and the careful inside is the positioning.

The headline formula

Claim, then twist: "Your roadmap is a wishlist. Strategy is subtraction."

Rules

Cadence

One long-form teardown per month. Zero hot takes. The restraint is part of the brand — publish the stat proudly.

Structure

Every essay answers three questions: what was known, what was chosen, what it cost.

Punctuation

No exclamation marks. No emoji in professional assets. Sentence case everywhere, including headlines.

Banned words

Game-changing, revolutionary, seamless, leverage, unlock, empower, journey, passionate. Say what happened instead.
