## Your people already use AI. Your business does not, yet.

More than half of Norwegian companies say they use AI. Very few have it in their
core processes. Employees use it on their own, pilots stay pilots, and the
licence bill grows faster than the results.

The problem is rarely the model. It is the data behind it, the operations nobody
costed, and the fact that nobody owns the decision when the system gets it wrong.

I help you take **one AI use case from pilot to production**, with a named person
behind every decision that cannot be undone.

## Is this you?

- You have AI pilots or licences, but nothing that changed how the business runs.
- Your developers use AI, but delivery is not noticeably faster.
- Your board or leadership asks *who is responsible* for what AI does, and nobody
  has a clear answer.

## Three ways to start

### Pilot to production review

*Two weeks.* We pick the one use case worth shipping, find what blocks it, and you get a
written plan your own team can run, with me or without me.

### AI in the delivery

*One working day.* How your team uses AI across specification, code, review, testing and operations,
without producing more code than anyone can review. Built on what we do at Izy,
where AI helped us replace a 150,000 kroner a year licence in a few evenings.

### The sleep test for leadership teams

*Half a day.* Map every point of no return where AI writes, decides or acts. Put a name on each.
See where GDPR and the AI Act touch you. Based on
[the sleep test](/sleep-test/), first published in
[Dagens Perspektiv](https://www.dagensperspektiv.no/synspunkt/ki-mister-ikke-nattesovnen-det-er-problemet/1465374).

## Why me

I run AI in production, not in slides. At Izy I lead the backend and the AI layer
behind hundreds of thousands of transactions a month: computer vision
self-checkout and demand forecasting, live in Norwegian commercial buildings.
Eight years across backend, machine learning and product, and formal training in
AI ethics.

## Questions

**Do you still work at Izy?**

Yes. I am Technical Product Manager at Izy, and I take a small number of
engagements alongside it. I do not take work that competes with Izy.

**Do you build, or only advise?**

Both. The recommendations come from someone who runs AI in production, not just
specifies it.

**What does the EU AI Act mean for us right now?**

Norway follows the Act through the EEA. The EU has agreed to push the main
high-risk deadlines to late 2027, while transparency duties come sooner. That is
time to prepare, not a reason to wait: knowing where AI makes decisions in your
business is the first step either way.
